# LABSA Brand Guide

Read this file before using the brand assets in the storefront.

## Approved logo files
- `public/brand/labsa-logo-reference.jpg` is the original, unmodified image supplied by the project owner. Keep it as the source reference.
- `public/brand/labsa-logo.png` is a high-resolution, tightly cropped version with a white background.
- `public/brand/labsa-logo.webp` is the optimized web asset made from the same crop. Use this for the storefront when supported.
- These are raster assets, not vector artwork. Do not describe them as SVG or redraw them as a different logo.

## What the logo depicts
The upper lettering spells `LABSA` through clothing silhouettes, not ordinary type with a separate clothing icon:
- `L` is folded trousers, with visible pocket, seam, and cuff details.
- The first `A` is orange overalls with straps and a bib pocket.
- `B` is a dark tailored jacket with lapels and pockets.
- `S` is a patterned scarf with fringed ends.
- The final `A` is an olive hoodie with a hood and front pouch.
- Sewing details such as thread, a needle, stitches, and buttons support the handmade clothing theme.
- Below the illustrations, the Arabic wordmark is `لبسة` and the tagline is `لبسة تليق بيك`.

## Visual direction and colors
- Background: pure white for the complete logo lockup.
- The artwork uses charcoal/near-black outlines, muted olive fabric, terracotta orange, and warm cream textile highlights.
- Approximate palette sampled from the supplied image: charcoal `#30302E`, olive `#808060`, terracotta `#C8784C`, warm fabric highlight `#E9D7BF`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- The original image is the color and detail source of truth. Keep its subtle texture, stitched outlines, and illustrated character; do not flatten it into a generic geometric wordmark.

## Usage rules
- Preserve the full logo, white background, and aspect ratio. Do not crop away the garment letters, Arabic name, or tagline.
- Use `labsa-logo.webp` for normal storefront display and `labsa-logo.png` as the high-resolution fallback.
- The logo is detailed; do not shrink the complete lockup until its Arabic name and tagline become unreadable. If a compact header treatment is needed, use a legible Arabic text wordmark and retain the supplied full logo elsewhere on the page. Do not invent a new symbol.
- Keep the Arabic spelling exactly as provided and render Arabic UI content in RTL.
